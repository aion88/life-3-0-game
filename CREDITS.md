## Inspiration

The game is inspired by *Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence* by Max Tegmark (2017), and by the "AI Aftermath Scenarios" cheatsheet published by the Future of Life Institute (Ariel Conn, 2017): https://futureoflife.org/ai/ai-aftermath-scenarios/

Max Tegmark, Ariel Conn and the Future of Life Institute are sources of inspiration. They are not authors of this game and have not endorsed it. Short quotations from the cheatsheet are attributed where they appear in the in-game atlas.
